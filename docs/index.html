Weltschmerz build nine

A job board promises to tell you what is open and whether you fit. Over five weeks it handed this search 1,908 listings.

It showed a closing date for 35 of them, an advert we could actually read for 185, and led to 1 interview.

Limit to respect
  • One candidate, one region, five weeks. This is not a market study.
  • A missing closing date means we could not read one, not that the board had none.
  • Aggregates only. No company names, no links, no notes are published.
